=======================================================
MEMENTO - Sample Photos Folder
=======================================================

You can place your photos (.jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp) directly into this folder!

How it works:
1. Drop any image files here (e.g., photo1.jpg, vacation.png).
2. Go to your MEMENTO web app in your browser (http://localhost:5173).
3. Click "Sync Sample Folder" on the sidebar or refresh the page.
4. Your new photos will automatically appear in your gallery and timeline!

You can also upload photos directly using the "Upload Photos" button in the web app.
